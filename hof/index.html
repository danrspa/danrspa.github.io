Whispers of the North

Deep summer breathes, whispers arise,
A typhoon's embrace, vast skies collide.
Lost in the dance, the moon's quiet gaze,
Designing circuits, life's intricate maze.
Communication flows, a social thread,
In winter's frost, the old tales tread.
Yet warmth endures, a fire's warm glow,
Жизнь всегда найдёт путь домой.
