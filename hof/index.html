Ashen March

The metal sky has cracked, a storm brews.
The harvest fields lie bruised beneath a blood‑red breath.
Shadows of the blade advance, unbound, relentless.
The earth drinks the ash, its channels of flame cooled.
Night's heavy cloak wraps the forest, no star to guide.
The hum of metal gears turns into a silent dirge.
Unseen armies march through fallen leaves, their purpose hidden.
The realm holds breath, awaiting the rise of new dawn.
