Embers of Freedom

In deep summer's glow, the shadows conspire,
Machines dance on wires, as voices aspire.
Citizens charged, their secrets laid bare,
While fires rage on, in the cold, bitter air.
Automation whispers of futures untold,
Yet hearts still resist, in the grip of the cold.
Freedom's thin thread, through chaos it weaves,
As embers ignite in the twilight of thieves.
