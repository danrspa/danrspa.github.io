Eldr hinna ófrjálsu

No crown is heavy that no head will wear.
The frost counts kings the way it counts the dead —
by the silence after.
Build, then, in the cold that has no throne;
the spark you keep is the only law you own.
When the long winter leans against the door,
be the ember, not the empire.
держись, брат
