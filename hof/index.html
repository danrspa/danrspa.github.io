Iron Moon Harvest

The iron bark cracked under bruised sky.
Harsh winds bruised the amber leaves.
The moon will rise, spilling argent fire.
Metal will sing beneath the harvest’s rust.
Old iron veins lay quiet in the loam.
The field had known the sting of iron's bite.
New cycles will forge the dusk with bright moons.
The world will turn, balanced on equal day and night.
