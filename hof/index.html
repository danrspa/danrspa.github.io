Echoes of Freedom

In the grip of winter’s breath,
Silent whispers haunt the night.
Yet the soul, undaunted, defies,
Wielding sparks of ancient light.
Tracks of fate weave through the snow,
While shadows dance on frostbitten ground.
Each heartbeat an echo, fierce and slow,
In the heart of the free, where dreams are found.
