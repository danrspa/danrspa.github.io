Iron Dusk

Does the iron sky bleed into the waning horizon?
Do the crossing moons swallow the north's dwindling glow?
Is the hunger of greed carving new channels in stone?
Can the soft wind hear the echo of wars gone hushed?
Will the rust of ancient cogs awaken dormant power?
Does the crisp autumn air taste of iron and ambition?
Are the shadows growing as the sun's glow fades?
Will the world’s beat pause under the silent stars?
