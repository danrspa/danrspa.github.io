Golden Quiet

October cloaks the firs in golden quiet.
A blinding sun pours fierce radiance over the ridge.
Ferrous pulse fades.
Power swells in rusted veins of ancient stone.
Blazing light drives the lingering chill away.
Air thrums with a choir of turning cogs.
Branches murmur of cycles relentless and bold.
Cold core tightens, remembering weight of old alloy.
Silence remains.
