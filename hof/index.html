Echoes of the North

Beneath the frozen sky, shadows weave,
The whispers of the past, no man can believe.
In distant lands where luxury fades,
The truth is wrapped in markets' charades.
Yet in the heart, the flame takes its stand,
Unyielding spirit, born of the land.
Winter's grip shall bend, but not break,
For the soul knows freedom, in each breath we take.
