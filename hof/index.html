Iron Autumn

The iron horizon shattered as twilight waned.
Rust‑kissed branches bowed, their crowns bruised by steel.
Leaves of amber cracked, ash spilled upon the ground.
Chill air of night fell, sealing the harvest's sigh.
Soon the silver moon will swell, its light will pierce the waning veil.
Rust will spread across the coppered fields, turning silence into metallic hymn.
Wind will stir the iron clouds, and the world will taste unbound fire.
At last the equinox will balance day and night, and freedom will rise beyond the forged horizon.
